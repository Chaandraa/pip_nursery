Chapter 05 • The Origin Chronicle / Midnight Memories

How Pip Became a Dream Engineer

Act I

A Quiet Night in Oakhaven

Far below the clouds, the little town of Oakhaven lay wrapped in heavy winter blankets. Yet up in the nursery tower, a baby with a smooth round skull face could not sleep: the silence felt too big for small ears.

Act II

The Spark of Invention

With clumsy little fingers, Pip assembled scraps of starlight and a winding music cylinder. When the key turned with a soft click-whirrr, glowing ribbons danced in tempo with a gentle pentatonic chime.

Act III

The First Delivery & Badge

Pip tied on a starry nightcap with a neat pink bow, boarded a wandering cloud, and delivered the first dream of floating safely on marshmallows. The Midnight Council proudly conferred the title: Junior Dream Engineer.
